About me

Hands-on engineer, from firmware to front desk

I'm a Systems Engineer in the Systems Assembly department at Aflak Electronic Industries Co. Ltd (Sudair Industrial and Business City, Saudi Arabia), where I've worked since 2018 across IT support, R&D electronics and end-to-end delivery of specialized systems.

My core work is the Car Parking Smart Kiosk Suite: entry, payment and exit kiosks with QR/RFID ticketing, NFC and mobile payments (Mada, STC Pay, Apple Pay, Visa), session tracking and full technical documentation — from wiring and firmware recovery of Android motherboards to the payment-terminal integration.

Beyond the factory floor I build my own tools: Windows desktop applications for production planning, Android applications, and IT-support utilities — and I document everything I ship.
